# Debate Workflow

## Core Debate Loop

```text
⚔️ Debate Round 1
        |
        v
🔄 Cross Challenge
        |
        v
🛡️ Rebuttal
        |
        v
⚔️ Debate Round 2
        |
        v
🔄 Cross Challenge
        |
        v
🛡️ Rebuttal
        |
        v
⚖️ Final Evaluation
        |
        v
🏆 Final Verdict
```

## Agent Roles

### VC Agent
Acts like a skeptical venture capitalist and challenges investment assumptions.

### Financial Agent
Examines revenue, costs, profitability and financial risk.

### Market Agent
Examines customers, market opportunity, competition and demand.

### Devil's Advocate
Attacks assumptions and searches for reasons the proposal could fail.

### Final Evaluator
Reviews the debate evidence and produces the final assessment.

The challenge/rebuttal stages make later reasoning responsive to earlier arguments.
