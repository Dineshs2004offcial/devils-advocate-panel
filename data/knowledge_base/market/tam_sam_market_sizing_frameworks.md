# TAM, SAM, SOM Market Sizing & Validation Framework

## Definitions & Rigor Standards
1. **Total Addressable Market (TAM)**:
   - Top-Down: Total annual spend across the entire macroeconomic category.
   - Bottom-Up (VC Preferred): `Total Potential Customer Accounts × Annual Contract Value (ACV)`.
2. **Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM)**:
   - The specific sub-segment of TAM that directly matches your current geography, platform, and vertical focus.
3. **Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM)**:
   - Realistic market share capture within a 3-5 year horizon (typically 2% - 10% of SAM depending on competition).

## Common Devil's Advocate Pitfalls
- **"The 1% of China / India" fallacy**: Claiming a massive TAM by asserting you only need 1% of an enormous population without distribution proof.
- **Overestimating Willingness to Pay**: Assuming non-paying users will convert to enterprise price points.
- **Ignoring Incumbent Response**: Incumbents (Microsoft, Salesforce, Google) bundling similar capabilities into existing enterprise enterprise tiers for free.
