# Seed & Series A Funding Milestones & Expectations

## Seed Stage Benchmarks
- **Traction**: Working MVP with 5-20 active beta/design partners; initial non-zero ARR ($10k - $100k) or strong open-source developer velocity (> 1,000 GitHub stars).
- **Valuation / Round Size**: $2M - $4M raised at $10M - $18M post-money valuation.
- **Runway Target**: 18 - 24 months to reach Series A milestones.

## Series A Readiness Criteria
- **Revenue**: $1M - $2.5M ARR growing > 2.5x YoY.
- **Customer Quality**: At least 5-10 enterprise contracts with ACV > $25k and negative net churn.
- **Distribution Engine**: Proven repeatable go-to-market playbook (inbound funnel, outbound SDR/AE quotas, self-serve product-led growth).
- **Founding Team**: Strong technical founders paired with domain-expert commercial leadership.
