# SaaS Unit Economics & Financial Benchmarks (2025-2026)

## Key Venture Capital Metrics
- **LTV / CAC Ratio**: Target > 3.0x for healthy SaaS. Below 2.0x indicates unsustainable acquisition costs.
- **CAC Payback Period**: Target < 12 months for SMB SaaS; < 18 months for Enterprise SaaS.
- **Net Revenue Retention (NRR)**:
  - Top Quartile Enterprise: > 120%
  - Median SaaS: 102% - 108%
  - Warning Zone: < 95%
- **Gross Margin**:
  - Pure Software / SaaS: 75% - 85%
  - AI Application Layer (with model inference cost): 60% - 72%
  - Services / Tech-Enabled: 40% - 55%
- **Magic Number (Sales Efficiency)**:
  - `(Quarterly ARR Growth * 4) / Prior Quarter Sales & Marketing Spend`
  - Score > 1.0: Accelerate sales spend
  - Score 0.75 - 1.0: Healthy efficiency
  - Score < 0.5: Poor efficiency, fix product-market fit or churn

## Rule of 40 Benchmark
- `YoY Revenue Growth Rate (%) + Free Cash Flow Margin (%) >= 40%`
- Seed / Early Stage: Focus on > 100% YoY growth with controlled burn multiple (< 1.5x).
