# VC Due Diligence: Moats, Defensibility & Fatal Red Flags

## 7 Powers / Defensibility Moats (Hamilton Helmer Framework)
1. **Network Effects**: Value to user increases with total network scale (marketplace, protocol).
2. **Switching Costs**: High friction or data gravity making vendor changes painful.
3. **Scale Economies**: Per-unit production/inference costs drop dramatically at scale.
4. **Counter-Positioning**: Incumbent cannot copy without damaging their core cash-cow business model.
5. **Process Power**: Deep proprietary operational know-how accumulated over time.
6. **Brand**: Trust and emotional affinity lowering CAC and sustaining pricing power.
7. **Cornered Resource**: Exclusive access to proprietary data, patents, or talent.

## Fatal Investment Red Flags
- **Thin AI Wrapper**: Simple UI over a commodity LLM prompt without proprietary fine-tuning, workflow integration, or proprietary system of record data.
- **Concentrated Customer Risk**: > 40% of revenue coming from a single customer account.
- **High Churn / Leaky Bucket**: Monthly logo churn > 3% on SMB or > 0.8% on Enterprise.
- **Single Point of Failure Regulatory Exposure**: Heavy dependence on unverified regulatory exemptions.
