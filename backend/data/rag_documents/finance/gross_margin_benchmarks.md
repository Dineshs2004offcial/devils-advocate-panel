# SaaS Gross Margin Benchmarks (2026)

## Overall Benchmark
Per the SaasRise 2026 SaaS Benchmark Report (2,500-company synthesis): median SaaS gross margin across all B2B SaaS is 75%. This splits meaningfully by delivery model:
- Pure SaaS (software-only delivery): 82% median gross margin
- SaaS + Services (bundled implementation/professional services): 72% median gross margin

## Public Company Reference Points
- Salesforce: subscription and support revenue is 93% of total revenue; overall company gross margin is not separately broken out in the cited sources but the mix skews heavily toward high-margin subscription revenue.
- ZoomInfo: 87.0% gross margin (FY2025, per its 10-K) — notably higher than the 75% cross-industry median, consistent with a pure-data/software business with minimal services component.
- HubSpot: not explicitly cited in gathered sources, but professional services and other revenue was only ~$58M of $2.6B total FY2024 revenue (~2%), implying a software-mix gross margin likely well above the 75% median as well.

## Why Gross Margin Matters for CAC Payback and LTV
Gross margin is the multiplier that converts revenue-based payback calculations into cash-based reality: a 75% gross margin company only recovers 75 cents of cash for every dollar of revenue booked. When gross margin drifts below roughly 75% and CAC payback simultaneously exceeds 18 months, the combination is flagged by 2026 SaaS-metrics commentary as a "cash trap" warning sign that investors are explicitly screening for — a company is effectively borrowing against future revenue to fund current growth without the margin cushion to make that sustainable.

## AI-Cost Caveat
2026 commentary specifically flags that AI-core SaaS products can post stronger overall efficiency scores (e.g., Rule of 40) even with lower gross margins, because faster AI-driven growth can offset compute-cost margin drag — meaning a gross margin somewhat below the 75-82% pure-SaaS benchmark is not automatically a red flag for an AI-native product, provided growth and Rule of 40 performance compensate.

Sources: SaasRise 2026 SaaS Benchmark Report; ZoomInfo FY2025 Form 10-K (SEC EDGAR); Beancount.io "2026 SaaS Metrics Stack" guide (CAC payback/gross margin interaction); HubSpot FY2025 10-K (revenue mix, cross-referenced).
