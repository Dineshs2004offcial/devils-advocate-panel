# SaaS CAC & LTV Benchmarks (2026)

## Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) by Segment
Per OpenView Partners SaaS Benchmarks (2025) as synthesized by StealthAgents research (2026):
- Median SMB-focused SaaS CAC: $1,100-$1,400
- Enterprise-focused SaaS CAC: $14,000-$22,000 — a 15x+ spread driven almost entirely by sales cycle length and headcount rather than product differences.

## CAC by GTM Motion (2026, SaaSHero benchmarking)
- Product-Led Growth (PLG): median CAC ~$702, ~15-18 month payback, S&M as ~28% of revenue
- Sales-Assisted/Hybrid: CAC ranges $500-$20,000 depending on ACV, payback under 12 months when PQL-sourced, ~45% blended S&M-to-revenue
- Sales-Led/Enterprise: median CAC ~$11,400, 15-18 month payback, ~45% S&M-to-revenue
A separate 2026 estimate puts overall B2B SaaS CAC at roughly $2 for every $1 of ARR generated in the first year — a rough "acquisition costs about double the first year's revenue" heuristic.

## CAC Payback Period Benchmarks
- Under 12 months: elite, especially for SMB-focused SaaS
- 12-18 months: median for B2B SaaS in 2026
- 18-24 months: acceptable for enterprise sales motions
- 24+ months: sustainability concern; expect investor pushback
Gross margin materially affects the "real" payback period — a 75% gross margin company only recovers 75 cents of cash per dollar of revenue booked, so payback-period math should always be margin-adjusted, not based on raw revenue.

## Lifetime Value (LTV) Context
LTV is typically calculated as (ARPA × Gross Margin) ÷ Customer Churn Rate. Because churn and gross margin vary enormously by segment (see the dedicated Churn/Retention and Gross Margin benchmark documents), LTV figures are only meaningful when paired with their churn and margin assumptions — a headline "$50K LTV" claim is not evaluable without those inputs.

## Framing Implication
Enterprise-focused sales-led motions have CAC roughly 10-15x higher than PLG motions, but typically also carry higher ACV and lower churn — the LTV:CAC ratio (see dedicated document) is the metric that actually normalizes this comparison, not CAC or LTV in isolation.

Sources: OpenView Partners SaaS Benchmarks 2025 (via StealthAgents "SaaS Startup Metrics Statistics 2026"); SaaSHero "2026 B2B SaaS CAC Benchmarks"; Beancount.io "2026 SaaS Metrics Stack" guide.
