# SaaS LTV:CAC Ratio Benchmarks (2026)

## The Textbook Benchmark vs. Reality
The commonly cited "healthy" LTV:CAC ratio benchmark is 3:1. However, per SaaS Capital's 2025 Annual Survey, median-performing SaaS companies in 2025 were actually running at 2.1:1 — meaningfully below the textbook target. Top-quartile companies averaged 4.5:1.

## Ratio by Business Model
Per the SaasRise 2026 Benchmark Report (2,500-company synthesis):
- All private SaaS companies: 3.3x median
- Bootstrapped companies: 5.0x median (notably higher — bootstrapped companies cannot subsidize inefficient acquisition with venture capital, so surviving bootstrapped companies are self-selected for capital efficiency)
- Enterprise sales-led companies: 3.0x median

## Elite/Target Benchmarks Cited by GTM Consultancies
Some 2026 sources push the "target" ratio considerably higher for scale-up-stage companies — e.g., SaaSHero cites 5:1+ as the target for capital-efficient customer acquisition at scale-up stage, and Kruze/CFO-advisor sources treat 3:1 as merely "acceptable" rather than "good" in the current tighter-capital environment.

## Why the Ratio Alone Is Insufficient
A high LTV:CAC ratio combined with a long CAC payback period (e.g., 24+ months) still creates a "cash gap" that limits reinvestment capacity even if the ratio looks healthy on paper — LTV:CAC and CAC payback period should always be read together, not as substitutes for one another (see the CAC/LTV benchmarks document for payback-period detail).

## Practical Application for a Fundraising Deck
Given that the *median* SaaS company in 2025 ran at 2.1:1 (below the "textbook" 3:1), a company presenting an LTV:CAC ratio anywhere above roughly 2.5:1-3:1 has grounds to describe itself as at or above the current market median — but claiming "healthy" performance requires citing which of these several benchmark sources is being used, since they diverge by up to 2.4x (2.1x median vs. 5.0x bootstrapped-company benchmark).

Sources: SaaS Capital 2025 Annual Survey (via StealthAgents "SaaS Startup Metrics Statistics 2026"); SaasRise 2026 SaaS Benchmark Report; SaaSHero SaaS metrics guides (2026).
