# SaaS Churn & Retention Benchmarks (2026)

## Gross Churn by Segment
Per ChartMogul's 2026 SaaS Benchmarks (via StealthAgents synthesis): median gross revenue churn for SMB-targeting SaaS is 11-13% annually; enterprise-focused companies run materially lower at 5-7% annually. The gap is attributed almost entirely to customer size and contract length rather than product-quality differences — a smaller/shorter-contract customer base is structurally higher-churn regardless of product satisfaction.

## Annual Account Churn by Segment (SaasRise 2026, broader sample)
- SMB: 15%
- Mid-Market: 10%
- Enterprise: 5%

## Net Revenue Retention (NRR)
- All B2B SaaS median: 102-105% (sources vary slightly)
- SMB: 100%
- Mid-Market: 104-106%
- Enterprise: 115-120%+
NRR above 120% is described as the single strongest predictor of long-term SaaS company value by Bessemer Venture Partners' State of the Cloud 2025 — companies with NRR above 120% reportedly command a 2-3x higher revenue multiple than companies with NRR below 100%.

## Elite/Target Benchmarks
Some 2026 GTM-consultancy sources set a higher bar for "good" performance: NRR of 120%+ and monthly churn below 3% as targets for companies seeking strong investor confidence (SaaSHero); 130%+ NRR is cited elsewhere as an enterprise-segment "great" benchmark (Claap.io SaaS Sales Metrics 2026).

## Churn Formula Nuance
Simple churn = (Customers lost in period ÷ Customers at start of period) × 100. A more revenue-accurate formula incorporates ARPA and gross margin: (ARPA × Gross Margin) ÷ Customer Churn Rate feeds into LTV calculations (see LTV:CAC document) — raw customer-count churn and revenue churn can diverge significantly if larger or smaller accounts churn disproportionately.

## Framing Implication
Because "churn" is reported inconsistently (gross vs. net, customer-count vs. revenue, monthly vs. annual) across sources, any churn figure presented in a fundraising context should specify all four dimensions explicitly to be comparable to the benchmarks above.

Sources: ChartMogul SaaS Benchmarks 2025 (via StealthAgents synthesis); SaasRise 2026 SaaS Benchmark Report; Bessemer Venture Partners State of the Cloud 2025 (NRR-multiple relationship); SaaSHero and Claap.io 2026 SaaS metrics guides.
