# SaaS ARR/MRR Growth Benchmarks (2026)

## Revenue Growth Benchmarks by Segment
Per the SaasRise 2026 SaaS Benchmark Report (synthesizing data from 2,500 private and public SaaS companies across 15+ industry surveys):
- Median SaaS revenue growth overall: 22% YoY
- SMB-segment median growth: 28% YoY
- Mid-market median growth: 22% YoY
- Enterprise median growth: 17% YoY
- AI-native companies: ~55% median revenue growth vs. ~14% for legacy SaaS peers — a striking divergence that should inform how "SaaS growth benchmark" comparisons are framed for an AI-enabled product.

## MRR/ARR Growth Rate Targets by Stage
Early-stage SaaS companies are commonly benchmarked against 15%+ monthly MRR/ARR growth rates (SaaSHero 2026 GTM benchmarks), though this compresses substantially as ARR base grows — a company at $50K MRR growing 15%/month is adding $7,500; the same growth rate at $2M MRR requires adding $300K/month, which is a fundamentally different execution challenge.

## Revenue Per Employee
$130K revenue per employee is typical for companies under $5M ARR, scaling to $300K+ for companies above $100M ARR (SaasRise 2026) — a useful efficiency benchmark for evaluating headcount-to-revenue ratios in a pitch deck.

## Average Contract Value / Revenue Concentration Context
For context from the competitive set: HubSpot's average subscription revenue per customer was $11,343 in FY2024 (essentially flat vs. prior year despite total revenue growth), illustrating that ARR growth driven primarily by new-logo volume rather than expansion is a real, observable pattern even at scale — not just an early-stage risk.

## Framing Note for Fundraising Decks
Because "SaaS growth benchmark" figures vary meaningfully by data source, segment definition, and time period, any pitch deck citing a specific growth-rate benchmark should name the source and segment explicitly (e.g., "22% median YoY growth for mid-market B2B SaaS, per SaasRise 2026") rather than presenting a single unsourced number as an industry-wide constant.

Sources: SaasRise 2026 SaaS Benchmark Report (2,500-company synthesis); SaaSHero "2026 B2B SaaS Metrics You Need to Track"; HubSpot FY2025 10-K (per-customer revenue figure, cross-referenced from competitor profile).
