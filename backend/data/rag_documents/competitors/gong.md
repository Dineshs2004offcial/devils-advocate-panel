# Competitor Profile: Gong

## Product
Revenue intelligence platform: records, transcribes, and analyzes sales calls/emails/meetings using AI to surface deal-risk signals, competitive mentions, and rep-coaching recommendations. Three core product lines: Conversation Intelligence, Deal Intelligence, and Forecast. Widely regarded as the revenue-intelligence category leader and was named a Leader in Gartner's inaugural Revenue Action Orchestration Magic Quadrant (Dec 2025).

## Target Customer
Mid-market to enterprise B2B sales organizations, particularly teams with 20+ reps closing $50K+ deals where call-volume and coaching ROI justify Gong's premium, non-transparent pricing.

## Business Model & Pricing
Notably opaque, multi-layer pricing rather than a single per-seat number:
- Per-user license: roughly $1,200-$2,500+/user/year depending on tier (Starter/SMB $1,200-1,600, Mid-Market $1,500-2,000, Enterprise $2,000-2,500+)
- Mandatory platform fee: $5,000-$50,000/year regardless of seat count (does not shrink for smaller teams)
- One-time onboarding/implementation: $7,500-$28,500
- 2025-2026 shift toward metered AI credits and module unbundling (Forecast, Engage, Insights sold as separate add-ons) has pushed effective per-user cost upward
A realistic Year 1 total for a 10-person team is roughly $21,000-$29,000; multi-year contracts often carry automatic 5-15% annual price uplifts.

## Strengths
Best-in-class conversation-intelligence depth with no close direct competitor at the same scale; coaching recommendations grounded in actual call data rather than manager observation; strong native integrations with Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoom, and Teams; dominant AI-visibility/mindshare position in the revenue-intelligence category.

## Weaknesses
Platform fee and onboarding costs do not scale down for smaller teams, making Gong disproportionately expensive below ~25 seats; pricing opacity is a widely-cited buyer frustration (Gong does not publish pricing); fundamentally reactive/backward-looking — it analyzes calls that have already happened rather than helping generate the pipeline or identify in-market buying signals upstream, a gap competitors (ZoomInfo, 6sense, Clay) are positioned to fill; total cost of ownership is reported to run 2-3x what functionally-similar competitors charge.

## Recent Positioning
2024-2025 launched Gong AI Agents; deepening competitive contrast against Salesloft, ZoomInfo's Chorus, and Outreach as Gartner's RAO category consolidates around unified platforms.
