# Competitor Profile: Salesloft

## Product
Salesloft Revenue Platform — evolved from a cadence/sequencing tool into a broader suite: Cadence (original sequencing engine), Conversations (call recording/intelligence), Deals (pipeline management), Drift (chat and intent, via acquisition), and Forecast. Named a Visionary in Gartner's inaugural Revenue Action Orchestration Magic Quadrant (Dec 2025).

## Target Customer
Enterprise and upper-mid-market sales engagement buyers; serves 5,000+ companies; direct head-to-head competitor to Outreach for the same enterprise buyer profile.

## Business Model & Pricing
Per-user pricing starting around $125/month, with realistic enterprise pricing landing at $150-$220/user/month — generally priced higher than Outreach's comparable $100-$160/user/month range, reflecting a "bundles more features but costs more for large teams" positioning per third-party comparisons. Salesloft's pricing model layers usage multipliers on top of base per-user fees (vs. Outreach's flatter per-user-plus-minimum-commitment structure).

## Ownership & Financials
Acquired by Vista Equity Partners in 2022 (going private). Total funding raised prior to acquisition: $246.07M across 13 rounds; December 2021 valuation (pre-Vista) was reported at $2.3B. 2025 revenue is reported at $450M (source: CB Insights financial tracking, though noted as a less-verified private-company estimate than the Vista-era deal terms). A separate, earlier 2023 estimate (Sacra) put Salesloft ARR at "$200M+."

## User Satisfaction
In third-party review aggregation (G2), Salesloft holds a higher average rating (4.5/5 across 4,264 reviews) than Outreach (4.3/5 across 3,531 reviews), with particular strength noted in ease of use, ease of setup, ease of admin, and quality of support — Salesloft users specifically cite superior call/email/text activity tracking versus Outreach's strength in interaction logging.

## Strengths
Broader, more integrated revenue-platform positioning (cadence + conversation intelligence + deals + intent in one system since the Drift acquisition); higher user-satisfaction scores across most G2 review categories; strong enterprise brand equity built over a decade as an Outreach co-leader in the category.

## Weaknesses
Higher realistic total cost of ownership than Outreach at comparable seat counts; going private under Vista Equity Partners in 2022 reduces financial transparency (no more public funding-round disclosures or independent ARR verification); usage-multiplier pricing structure can create unpredictable cost scaling for growing teams, a common buyer complaint versus flatter per-seat competitors.

Sources: LeadHaste "Salesloft vs Outreach.io"; CB Insights Salesloft financials page; G2 "Compare Outreach and Salesloft"; CostBench "Outreach vs Salesloft Pricing 2026"; Komo.ai (Gartner RAO MQ, Dec 2025).
