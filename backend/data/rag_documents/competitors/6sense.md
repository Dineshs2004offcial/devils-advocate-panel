# Competitor Profile: 6sense

## Product
AI-powered account-based marketing (ABM) and intent-data platform. Aggregates buying signals (website visits, email opens, review-site visits, and other "dark funnel" behavior) and applies predictive AI models to identify high-intent target accounts before they've engaged a vendor directly. Analyzes a reported 500 billion intent signals per month across a database of 449+ million buyer profiles.

## Founding Story
Founded 2013 in San Francisco by Amanda Kahlow, Dustin Chang, Viral Bajaria, Premal Shah, and Shane Moriah. Kahlow developed the founding insight after a Cisco consulting engagement on sales-process design.

## Target Customer
Mid-market and enterprise B2B revenue teams needing advanced ABM and intent-driven targeting; named customers include Cisco, NetApp, and Thomson Reuters. Serves sectors including business services, financial services, manufacturing, software/technology, and transportation/logistics.

## Business Model & Pricing
Subscription SaaS, customized/tiered pricing (Free, Team, Growth, Enterprise tiers), typically ranging from roughly $60,000 to over $100,000 per year for meaningful deployments — reflecting an enterprise-sales-led motion rather than Apollo's self-serve model. Land-and-expand strategy: customers typically increase spend over time by adding seats and capabilities, contributing to reportedly strong net dollar retention.

## Recent Financials
2023 ARR estimated at $210M (Sacra), up 40% YoY from ~$150M in 2022 — though a separate, earlier estimate put 2023 ARR at $111.3M (Craft.co, based on 2021 data updated). Scaled from $18M ARR in 2019 to over $200M by 2023. Valued at $5.2B following a 2022 Series E led by Blue Owl and MSD Partners — implying a rich ~25-35x forward revenue multiple depending on which ARR figure is used. Total funding raised: $526M (or $428M per a separate Dealroom tally) across multiple rounds including a $100M raise in mid-2023.

## Strengths
Category-defining position in AI-driven intent data and ABM — considered the leader ahead of Demandbase and newer AI-native ABM entrants; strong enterprise reference customers; expansion into adjacent capabilities (conversation intelligence, revenue intelligence) broadens its platform surface area beyond pure ABM.

## Weaknesses
High price point ($60K-$100K+/year) limits addressable market to mid-market/enterprise, ceding the SMB self-serve segment entirely to Apollo and similar PLG competitors; valuation multiple (25-35x ARR) is rich relative to peers and vulnerable to compression if growth decelerates from its 2022-2023 pace; competitive intensity has increased from both established players (Demandbase) and AI-native ABM startups.

Sources: Sacra "6sense" company research page; Contrary Research "6sense" report; Craft.co 6sense profile; Dealroom.co 6sense funding table; Spendflo and Vertice 6sense pricing pages.
