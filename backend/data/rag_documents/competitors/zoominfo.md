# Competitor Profile: ZoomInfo (NASDAQ: GTM)

## Product
Cloud-based go-to-market intelligence platform: B2B contact/company database plus AI-driven prospecting automation, buying-signal monitoring, and outreach personalization tools for sales, marketing, operations, and recruiting teams. Also owns Chorus, a conversation-intelligence product competing directly with Gong.

## Target Customer
Mid-market to enterprise revenue teams that historically bought lead-list data from data brokers (Dun & Bradstreet) and layered on email validation/list-segmentation tooling — ZoomInfo's original differentiation versus pure data brokers starting around 2010.

## Business Model & Pricing
Subscription/contract-based, historically sold as larger up-front annual contracts (~$9K+ historically cited as a reference point competitors like Apollo priced against) rather than Apollo's self-serve monthly plans — reflecting ZoomInfo's top-down, enterprise-sales-led GTM motion versus Apollo's bottom-up self-serve motion.

## Recent Financials (Public Company — SEC Filings)
FY2025 (year ended Dec 31, 2025) revenue: $1,249.5 million, up from $1,214.3 million in 2024 (+2.9% YoY) and $1,239.5 million in 2023 — notably, 2025 revenue only just exceeded 2023's level, indicating a multi-year growth plateau. FY2025 net income: $124.2 million (9.9% net margin). Operating margin: 18.1%; gross margin: 87.0%. Trading ticker changed to "GTM" (Nasdaq). Market cap/valuation context: at Q3 2023, ZoomInfo traded around $1.26B in annualized revenue at a ~4x forward multiple on a $5B market cap — a materially lower revenue multiple than high-growth private peers like Apollo (10-17x) or Outreach (17-25x at various points), reflecting the market's pricing of ZoomInfo's slower (single-digit) growth rate as a mature, public company.

## Strengths
Largest and most established B2B contact/company database in the category; public-company financial transparency and balance-sheet strength; owns both the data layer (ZoomInfo core) and a conversation-intelligence product (Chorus), giving it a broader stack than pure-play competitors.

## Weaknesses
Growth has plateaued (essentially flat 2023-2025 revenue) as its core data-broker business model faces commoditization pressure from AI-powered scraping tools and signal-based alternatives (Clay, Apollo); as a public company, its 4x forward revenue multiple versus double-digit multiples for high-growth private peers signals the market's view that ZoomInfo is now a mature, low-growth incumbent rather than a growth story — a material data point for any pitch positioning against it as "the slow incumbent."
