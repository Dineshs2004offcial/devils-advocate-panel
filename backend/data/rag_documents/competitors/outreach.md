# Competitor Profile: Outreach

## Product
"AI Revenue Workflow Platform" for sales engagement — cadence/sequencing across email, phone, and other channels, plus Kaia AI conversation intelligence, AI Smart Account Plans, and AI Coaching layered on over time. Positioned by Gartner as a Leader in the inaugural Revenue Action Orchestration Magic Quadrant (Dec 2025).

## Target Customer
Skews heavily enterprise (100+ seat deployments); serves 5,000-6,000+ customers including SAP, Siemens, Snowflake, Verizon, Zuora, ZoomInfo, and CenturyLink.

## Founding & Business Model
Founded 2014 in Seattle by Manny Medina, Gordon Hempton, Andrew Kinzer, and Wes Hather — originally built GroupTalent, a technical recruiting platform, before pivoting into enterprise sales tooling. Early growth targeted SMBs with $50-100/month plans as a cheaper alternative to ZoomInfo's ~$9K annual contracts; the company later removed its entry-level $40/month package in 2022 to focus upmarket on enterprise deals starting at $2,500/month. Current per-user pricing runs roughly $100-$160/month across three named plans (Amplify, Amplify Plus, Premier), though actual negotiated/median pricing (per Vendr deal-flow data) runs notably lower than list price.

## Recent Financials
2024 revenue reported at $300.8M ARR (up from $206.6M in 2023); other estimates (Sacra, based on 2023 data) put ARR at $250M in 2023 (11% growth from $225M in 2022). Valuation: $4.2-4.4B, based on a $201M Series G round led by Premji Invest in May 2021. Total funding raised: approximately $488.7-489M across 7 rounds, from investors including DFJ Growth, Lone Pine Capital, Sands Capital, Salesforce Ventures, and Mayfield Fund.

## Strengths
Category-leading conversation intelligence and forecasting accuracy; deep CRM bidirectional sync (Salesforce, MS Dynamics); strong enterprise logo base and reference customers; heavy, sustained AI-feature investment (Kaia).

## Weaknesses
Growth has decelerated notably (11% YoY in 2023 vs. 100%+ YoY growth in earlier years like 2016-2021), suggesting enterprise-segment saturation pressure; simpler/predictable pricing than Salesloft is also a double-edged sword — it signals less bundled value for large accounts; implementation for enterprise deployments typically runs 12-16 weeks given integration complexity, a real adoption friction point relative to faster-to-deploy PLG competitors like Apollo.

## Direct Competitive Set
Salesloft (closest peer — both enterprise-focused sequencing/engagement platforms), Apollo.io (lower-cost, faster-growing PLG entrant), Gong/Clari (adjacent revenue-intelligence layer).
