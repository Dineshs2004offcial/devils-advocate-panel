# B2B SaaS Market Trends Report (2026)

## Macro Shift: Growth to Efficient Growth
The single largest trend shaping B2B SaaS in 2026 is the shift from "growth at all costs" (2021-2022) to capital-efficient growth. Efficient growth now commands a 20-30% premium on ARR multiples, and companies consistently clearing the "Rule of 40" threshold trade at roughly 2x the revenue multiple of less-efficient peers. Median Rule of 40 scores for private B2B SaaS companies with $5M-$30M ARR sit around 25-28 in 2025/2026 (down from 32 in 2022), with only 9% of sub-$30M-ARR companies clearing the 40% threshold outright.

## Pricing Model Transition
Per-seat pricing — the default model for a generation of SaaS — is in structural decline. Roughly 58% of SaaS products still use per-seat/per-user pricing, but 42% now offer a usage-based/consumption option (up from 27% in 2023), and 85% of surveyed SaaS companies in a January 2025 Metronome/Greyhound Capital survey already had some usage-based pricing live. 73% of vendors now charge separately for AI features ("AI surcharges"). There is an emerging counter-trend: Salesforce's Marc Benioff has publicly pushed back toward "predictable" per-user pricing after customer pushback on per-conversation/credit-based AI pricing.

## AI-Native vs. Legacy Divergence
AI-native SaaS companies are reported to post median revenue growth of ~55% vs. ~14% for legacy SaaS peers (SaasRise 2026 Benchmark Report, synthesizing 2,500 companies). AI-native companies also command materially higher M&A revenue multiples (10-15x+ vs. 3.8x for typical private SaaS, 4.8x for bootstrapped).

## Segment Benchmarks (SaasRise 2026, 2,500-company sample)
- Median SaaS revenue growth: 22% YoY overall (SMB 28%, Mid-Market 22%, Enterprise 17%)
- Net Revenue Retention: 102% median overall (SMB 100%, Mid-Market 104%, Enterprise 115%)
- SaaS gross margin: 75% median overall (Pure SaaS 82%, SaaS+Services 72%)
- Revenue per employee: $130K (sub-$5M ARR) scaling to $300K+ (above $100M ARR)

## Implication for Positioning
A fundraising narrative in 2026 needs to lead with efficiency metrics (burn multiple, CAC payback, NRR) alongside growth rate — growth alone is no longer a sufficient story for institutional investors.
