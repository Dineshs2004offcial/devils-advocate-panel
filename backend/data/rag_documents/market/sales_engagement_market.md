# Sales Engagement Platform Market Report

## Market Size
Estimates vary by research firm depending on category scope. Persistence Market Research and related 2026 press coverage size the global sales engagement platform market at approximately $9.2 billion in 2026, projected to reach $26.6 billion by 2033 (16.3-16.4% CAGR). A separate 2026 estimate (Jeeva AI, citing broader "sales-engagement software" spend) puts the market at $7.5 billion in 2024 growing to $18.4 billion by 2033 (10.6% CAGR), with the US market alone growing from $1.5B to $4.5B (15.2% CAGR). These figures diverge because vendors define the category differently (pure sequencing tools vs. the broader engagement + intelligence + forecasting stack) — treat both as directional rather than authoritative.

## Category Shift: From "Sales Engagement" to "Revenue Action Orchestration"
In December 2025, Gartner retired its long-standing "Sales Engagement Applications" Magic Quadrant category and published an inaugural Magic Quadrant for Revenue Action Orchestration (RAO). This reflects the convergence of engagement (cadence/sequencing), revenue/conversation intelligence, and pipeline forecasting into a single AI-driven layer. Outreach, Clari, and Gong were named Leaders in the inaugural RAO MQ; Salesloft was named a Visionary.

## Buyer Behavior
RAIN Group research covering $4.2 billion in purchases across 488 buyers and 489 sellers found it takes an average of 8 touchpoints to book an initial B2B meeting. Structured sales engagement adoption is associated with a 25% average increase in sales productivity and 15% revenue growth, per Persistence Market Research's 2026 analysis.

## Pricing Range
Typical seat pricing across the category runs roughly $49-$165/seat/month depending on tier and vendor, though enterprise revenue-intelligence tools (e.g., Gong) layer platform fees and onboarding costs on top of per-seat pricing (see the Gong competitor profile).

## Key Players
Outreach, Salesloft, Apollo.io, Gong, Clari, HubSpot Sales Hub, ZoomInfo/Chorus, 6sense, Groove, Amplemarket, Klenty.

Sources: Persistence Market Research (2026, via press release); Jeeva AI "Future of Sales-Engagement AI" (2025); Komo.ai sales-engagement and sales-execution glossary pages, citing Gartner (Dec 2025 RAO Magic Quadrant) and RAIN Group research.
