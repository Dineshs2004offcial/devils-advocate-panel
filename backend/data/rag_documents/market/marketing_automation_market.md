# Marketing Automation Market Report

## Market Size
Estimates again diverge by source:
- Grand View Research: global marketing automation market reached $6.65B in 2024, projected to grow to $15.58B by 2030 (15.3% CAGR).
- A separate 2026 roundup (Statista/MarketsandMarkets-sourced) sizes the market at $8.4B in 2026, growing 12.3% CAGR to $13.7B by 2030 — a trajectory of $5.2B (2022) → $6.1B (2023) → $6.9B (2024) → $7.6B (2025) → $8.4B (2026).
North America holds an estimated 41% share, Europe 32%, and Asia-Pacific 21% of the global market.

## Adoption
76% of companies use some form of marketing automation (HubSpot State of Marketing 2026); other estimates put adoption at 80-90% of companies by end of 2025. Email automation is the largest use-case segment (36% of market spend or the most-automated channel at 58% of marketers), followed by lead management (24%) and social media automation.

## ROI
For every $1 spent on marketing automation, companies report earning $5.44 over three years on average, with 76% of adopters generating positive ROI within the first year. Automated emails generate 320% more revenue than non-automated emails. Nurtured leads make 47% larger purchases than non-nurtured leads, and nurture campaigns drive a reported 451% increase in qualified leads (Annuitas/Forrester-sourced figure).

## AI Layer
92% of marketers report using AI tools as part of marketing automation in 2025. AI-driven predictive lead scoring is reported to improve accuracy by ~52%, send-time optimization lifts open rates ~23%, and AI-personalized content improves engagement ~38% (Salesforce Einstein/HubSpot-sourced figures, treat as vendor-reported rather than independently audited).

## Relevance to Sales & Marketing SaaS Positioning
Marketing automation is adjacent to — and increasingly converging with — the CRM and sales-engagement categories (HubSpot's own product suite spans all three), making competitive boundaries increasingly blurred rather than sharply segmented.

Sources: Grand View Research (via SHNO.co roundup, 2026); SearchLab marketing automation statistics 2026 (citing Statista, MarketsandMarkets, Grand View Research, Forrester); SHNO.co "Marketing Automation with AI Statistics for 2026."
