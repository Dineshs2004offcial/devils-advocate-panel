# B2B SaaS Buyer & Adoption Research

## Product-Led Growth Adoption as a Buying-Behavior Signal
58% of B2B SaaS companies now run some form of product-led growth (PLG) motion, and 91% plan to increase PLG investment going forward (ProductLed 2025 benchmarks, 600+ companies) — this reflects a buyer-side shift toward self-serve evaluation before any vendor sales contact, not just a vendor-side GTM preference. 75% of PLG companies start the buyer relationship with a free trial or freemium model rather than a traditional demo-first sales process.

## Product-Qualified Leads (PQLs) Convert Far Better Than Traditional Leads
PQLs (leads that have demonstrated product usage/value before sales engagement) convert at 25-30%, roughly 3-5x better than traditional MQLs — a strong signal that buyers who have already experienced product value are dramatically easier to close than buyers evaluating from marketing content alone.

## The Buying Committee Problem
CRM adoption research indicates 90% of organizations consider CRM data "the cornerstone" of operations, yet 76% say less than half of their CRM data is accurate/complete — a data-quality gap that directly affects how B2B buying committees evaluate vendors, since inaccurate internal data undermines the ROI case buyers build internally before purchase.

## AI as a Purchase Criterion
90% of B2B software buyers report being more likely to choose a vendor with AI capabilities (2026 CRM-adoption research) — AI is no longer a differentiator but close to a baseline expectation in the buying decision, even though (per the Technology & AI documents) buyer skepticism about vague "AI-powered" claims is simultaneously rising.

## Hybrid GTM as the Emerging Buyer-Experience Norm
The "PLG vs. sales-led" framing is increasingly treated as a false choice on the buyer-experience side as well as the vendor GTM-strategy side: buyers increasingly expect to self-serve initial evaluation (trial/freemium) and only engage a human salesperson once they've independently validated value — a pattern consistent with the PQL conversion data above and with the shorter-trial/urgency findings in the Freemium vs. Free Trial document.

## Willingness-to-Pay Research Gap
As detailed in the dedicated WTP document, only 28% of SaaS vendors regularly research what buyers will actually pay — meaning most vendor pricing decisions are made without direct buyer-adoption research, a gap that sophisticated buyers (per the "buyer maturity" WTP finding) are increasingly capable of exploiting in negotiation.

Sources: Enrich Labs "Product-Led Growth: Complete Guide 2026" (citing ProductLed 2025 benchmarks); Cleverly.co "Product-Led Growth + Outbound Sales" (PQL conversion data); CRM market statistics (SLT Creative, Wavecnct 2026 roundups, cross-referenced from Market documents).
