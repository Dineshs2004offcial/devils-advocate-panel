# Freemium vs. Free Trial Research (2026)

## Headline Conversion Rates
Reported figures vary somewhat by source, but converge on a consistent pattern:
- Free trial (opt-in, no credit card): median 18.5% trial-to-paid conversion for B2B SaaS
- Free trial (opt-out, credit card required upfront): 31.4-48.8% conversion — significantly higher, but with correspondingly higher churn from involuntary/surprise conversions
- Freemium: median 2.6-3.7-8% conversion depending on source and time window (a 2026 Kyle Poyar/ChartMogul/ProductLed survey of 200 B2B products found the median sits around 8%, though a quarter of freemium products convert under 2.5% within six months, and another quarter converts 10-15%)

## Why Raw Conversion Rate Is the Wrong Comparison
Freemium and free-trial funnels have very different top-of-funnel volume: per Kyle Poyar's 2026 report, for every 1,000 website visitors, freemium products generate ~90 signups vs. only ~45 for free-trial products. Once signup-volume is factored in, freemium products can produce *more* paying customers per 1,000 visitors than free trials, despite freemium's lower raw conversion percentage — meaning conversion rate alone, without signup volume, understates freemium's effectiveness for products with strong top-of-funnel appeal.

## Trial Length Effects
A/B test data ("Science Says" study) found 7-day trials outperformed 30-day trials: +5.6% conversion, +6.4% retention, +7.9% revenue. The mechanism: shorter trials create urgency, while users disengaged by day 3 of a 30-day trial rarely convert on day 29. 14 days remains the most common industry-standard trial length, balancing urgency against adequate time-to-value for more complex products.

## The Human-Touchpoint Factor
ChartMogul's 2026 analysis of 200 B2B products found 80% of B2B SaaS products with free trials include a human touchpoint (in-app chat, sales call, or onboarding email sequence) — and this is specifically correlated with the best-performing companies pushing conversion rates above 25%, well above the 18.5% median.

## Model Selection Guidance
- Freemium works when time-to-value is under ~5 minutes and/or the product benefits from virality/network effects (Slack, Dropbox-style products).
- Free trial is favored when ACV exceeds roughly $500/year and the product requires setup, configuration, or integration — the trial's built-in deadline sorts serious buyers from casual signups more effectively than an open-ended free tier.
- Hybrid models (time-limited full-product trial, followed by a permanent freemium tier for non-converters) are increasingly used by sophisticated 2026 SaaS companies to capture both trial urgency and freemium's larger funnel.

Sources: Userpilot "Freemium SaaS" (citing Kyle Poyar/ChartMogul/ProductLed 2026 survey of 200 B2B products); Fungies.io "SaaS Free Trial vs Freemium: Which Model Converts Better in 2026?" (multiple related pages, citing ChartMogul 2026 analysis and "Science Says" trial-length study).
