# B2B SaaS Customer Acquisition Benchmarks

## CAC by GTM Motion (SaaSHero 2026)
| GTM Motion | Median CAC | Median Payback | S&M as % of Revenue |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product-Led Growth (PLG) | $702 | 15-18 months | ~28% |
| Sales-Assisted/Hybrid | $500-$20,000 (ACV-dependent) | Under 12 months (PQL-sourced) | ~45% blended |
| Sales-Led/Enterprise | ~$11,400 | 15-18 months | ~45% |
Below roughly $5K ACV, PLG is described as an "economic necessity" because sales-led CAC of $5K-$50K simply cannot pay back against that contract size.

## Segment-Based CAC (OpenView 2025)
Median SMB-focused SaaS CAC: $1,100-$1,400; enterprise-focused SaaS CAC: $14,000-$22,000 — a 15x+ spread driven almost entirely by sales-cycle length and headcount requirements rather than product differences.

## Funnel Conversion Benchmarks (2026)
- Visitor-to-lead conversion: industry median 1.5-4.3% depending on source; top 10% of companies achieve 8-15%, a 5-7x performance gap.
- MQL-to-SQL conversion: averages just 13% industry-wide (the largest single funnel choke point); enterprise SaaS with advanced lead scoring reaches 40%.
- SQL-to-closed-won: 18-25% typical range.
- Demo-to-close win rates: 20-30% average, with best-in-class reaching 35-40%.

## PLG vs. Sales-Led Funnel Comparison
| Funnel Stage | PLG Model | Sales-Led Model |
|---|---|---|
| Visitor-to-Trial/Demo | 8-12% | 2-4% |
| Close rate | ~15-20% | ~35% |
| Deal size | Smaller | Larger |
PLG converts visitors to trials at a meaningfully higher rate but closes a smaller share of those into paid deals at smaller average contract values; sales-led converts fewer visitors into initial engagement but closes a much higher share at larger deal sizes.

## Free-to-Paid Conversion Within PLG
Free-to-paid conversion averages 9% across PLG models generally; opt-out trials (credit card required) convert at 48.8% vs. 18.2% for opt-in trials — see the Freemium vs. Free Trial document for full detail on this distinction.

## ROAS Benchmark
B2B SaaS paid-acquisition advertising should target at least 4x ROAS for sustainable growth; agencies/campaigns below 3x typically signal poor targeting, weak creative, or broken campaign/attribution structure.

## Implication for GTM Strategy Selection
The data above supports a hybrid framing rather than a binary PLG-vs-sales-led choice: PLG for efficient top-of-funnel volume and low-ACV segments, sales-assisted conversion of product-qualified leads (PQLs) for mid-market, and dedicated enterprise sales motion above the ACV threshold where sales-led CAC can pay back (see Sales-Led vs. PLG document for the full strategic framework).
