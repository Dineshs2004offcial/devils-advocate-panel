# B2B SaaS Sales Funnel Benchmarks (2026)

## Full-Funnel Conversion Benchmarks
| Stage | Median | Elite/Top 10% |
|---|---|---|
| Visitor-to-Lead | 1.5-4.3% | 8-15% |
| MQL-to-SQL | 13% (median, largest choke point) | up to 40% (enterprise, advanced lead scoring) |
| SQL-to-Closed-Won | 18-25% | 35-40%+ |
| Demo-to-Close | 20-30% average | 35-40% best-in-class |

## CAC Payback Period by Funnel Stage Efficiency
Healthy B2B SaaS companies typically see CAC payback periods of 80-120 days when measured from a marketing/funnel-efficiency lens (a different framing than the company-wide 12-18 month CAC payback benchmark cited in the Finance documents, which includes fully-loaded sales costs); elite performers reach 60-80 days.

## Net Revenue Retention as a "Funnel" Metric
Expansion revenue (upsell/cross-sell within existing accounts) is increasingly treated as part of the overall revenue funnel rather than a separate motion: 106-120% NRR is considered a healthy benchmark, with 130%+ considered elite/enterprise-grade — meaning the "funnel" doesn't end at closed-won for a mature GTM analysis, it continues through expansion.

## Lead Velocity Rate (LVR)
LVR = ((This Month's Qualified Leads − Last Month's Qualified Leads) ÷ Last Month's Qualified Leads) × 100. Benchmark: above 15% month-over-month growth is healthy; above 20% is considered elite. LVR is cited as the strongest early leading indicator of future ARR growth, since it predicts pipeline before that pipeline converts to revenue.

## PLG vs. Sales-Led Funnel Divergence (repeated for funnel-specific context)
PLG funnels show materially higher top-of-funnel conversion (8-12% visitor-to-trial vs. 2-4% for sales-led) but lower close rates (15-20% vs. ~35% for sales-led) and smaller average deal sizes — see the Sales-Led vs. PLG document for the full strategic implications of this tradeoff.

## Attribution Challenge
A commonly cited 2026 execution gap is that many B2B SaaS companies still cannot connect ad/channel spend through to closed-won revenue in their CRM (rather than stopping at lead-count or MQL-count metrics) — multi-touch attribution tied to actual pipeline and revenue outcomes, rather than top-of-funnel vanity metrics, is repeatedly flagged as the differentiator between companies hitting the elite conversion benchmarks above and those stuck at industry medians.
