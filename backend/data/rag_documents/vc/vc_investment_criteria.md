# VC Investment Criteria (2026)

## Primary Screening Criteria
Per Qubit Capital's 2026 research on VC due-diligence practices, most VC firms apply a first-pass screen across four primary criteria before a deal reaches a partner's inbox:
1. Company stage
2. Geographic presence
3. Financial profile
4. Industry focus
With an estimated ~137,000 startups launching daily worldwide, aggressive early-stage screening is a survival necessity for VC deal teams, not a courtesy.

## Secondary Evaluation Factors
Once a startup clears the primary filters, VCs assess:
- Founding team quality (experience, track record, team cohesion)
- Market potential and traction (customer growth, revenue momentum, retention)
- Technology, intellectual property, and defensibility
- Unit economics and capital efficiency (burn multiple, LTV:CAC — see Finance documents)

## 2026-Specific Shifts
- Series A funds are now explicitly evaluating whether a company has "a codified, repeatable GTM," not merely early product-market fit — the bar has moved from "does PMF exist" to "can this scale predictably."
- Cybersecurity due diligence has become mainstream: an estimated 60% of VCs will require cybersecurity audits as part of evaluation by 2026, up sharply from historical norms — reflecting both AI-related data-handling risk and general enterprise-buyer expectations.
- AI-native companies face what one 2026 source calls a "binary" evaluation: either a 40-60% valuation premium for genuine AI-native economics, or outright disqualification if AI is judged to be a marketing veneer rather than a structural advantage.
- Deal-team time investment: VCs reportedly spend at least 20 hours per deal on due diligence on average, reflecting increasing rigor even at early stages.

## Cold-Start Difficulty
An estimated 2 of 3 Series A deals in 2025-2026 involve a founder the lead investor has known for 6-9+ months prior to the round — meaning "cold" Series A pitches with no prior investor relationship are a distinct minority of successful raises. This has significant implications for fundraising timeline planning: relationship-building should begin well before a formal raise process starts.

## Implication for a Fundraising Narrative
A pitch deck should proactively address unit economics (burn multiple, LTV:CAC, NRR — see Finance section) rather than leading with growth rate alone, and should be prepared to discuss data-security/AI-governance posture given the rising prevalence of cybersecurity due diligence.

Sources: Qubit Capital "What Venture Capital Due Diligence Actually Covers at Each Stage" and "What Criteria Do Major VC Firms Use in 2025?"; WaveUp "Series A Fundraising in 2026: The Rules of Survival."
